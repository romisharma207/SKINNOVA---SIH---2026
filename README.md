## 🚀 Problem Statement
Millions of people in India face skin problems but have:
- No access to dermatologists in rural areas
- No idea which product suits their skin
- Expensive consultation fees

## 💡 Our Solution: SKINNOVA
An AI-powered mobile app that provides:
1.  **Instant AI Skin Analysis** - Just click a photo
2.  **Personalized Product Recommendation** - Affordable & Indian skin friendly
3.  **Doctor Connect** - Connect with dermatologists if needed
4.  **Progress Tracking** - Track your skin health journey

- **Database:** Firestore
