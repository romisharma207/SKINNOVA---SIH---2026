# Problem Evidence - Why SKINNOVA is Needed?

## 📊 Evidence that the Problem is Real & Critical

### 1. Massive Search Demand & Awareness Gap
- 1.2M+ monthly searches in India for "skin disease treatment" (Source: Google Trends, 2024-25)
- 500K+ monthly searches for "pimple treatment"
- Proves high demand but lack of trusted solution

### 2. Severe Shortage of Dermatologists in India
- Only 1 Dermatologist per 100,000 people in India (WHO Report 2024)
- Rural areas have almost zero dermatologists; patients travel 80-100KM
- Average consultation fee: INR 500 - 1500, not affordable for low-income groups

### 3. Real User Pain Points (Primary Survey - 50 Respondents)
- 78% users: "We don't know which cream/product is right for our skin"
- 65% users: Damaged their skin by using wrong products suggested by social media
- 82% college students reported having acne but no proper guidance
- 90% want an instant AI-based first check before visiting a doctor

### 4. Secondary Research & News Evidence
- Economic Times (2024): "India faces 70% shortage of dermatologists"
- AIIMS Study: 30% of teenagers with chronic acne face low confidence & mental stress
- ISD Report: 60% of skin issues worsen due to late diagnosis

### 5. Our Validation
- Conducted Google Form Survey (Link available in user-survey.md)
- 1-on-1 Interviews with 5 users from rural areas near Pune

## 🎯 Conclusion
The problem is real, large-scale, and underserved. There is a clear gap between need and access to dermatology experts. SKINNOVA can bridge this gap with AI-powered instant analysis and affordable guidance.
